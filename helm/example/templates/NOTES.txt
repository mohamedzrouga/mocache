A rolling upgrade (helm upgrade) replaces one pod at a time (OrderedReady).
That pod's shard is empty until clients refill it. The other N-1 pods keep
serving. Clients must treat network errors as misses — there is no failover
to a different node.

Liveness is /livez (stays 200 during drain). Readiness is /readyz (goes 503
on SIGTERM so Endpoints drop the pod before sockets close).
